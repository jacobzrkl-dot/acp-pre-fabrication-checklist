# ACP Finish-Control Record

Use this record when a decorative aluminium composite panel (ACP) finish moves from design selection into procurement, fabrication, delivery and the first installed condition.

The purpose is traceability. It helps the project team keep the approved physical sample connected to the panel order and the current drawing revision. It does not approve a wall assembly, fixing design, fire strategy, substrate condition or installation method.

## 4. Comparison conditions

Agree how the project team will compare the production material with the reference sample.

- View the sample and production panel in the same orientation.
- Use a consistent light source and viewing distance.
- Remove or account for protective film before a final visual decision.
- Compare more than one panel when natural batch variation may be relevant.
- Record who attended, the date, and whether the review was at the factory, workshop or site.
- Keep subjective comments such as “warmer” or “darker” attached to a named observer and comparison condition.

If the project requires instrument readings or numerical tolerances, record the specified instrument, method, acceptance range and responsible party. Do not invent a tolerance after a visual disagreement occurs.

## 7. Close-out rule

Do not mark the finish record complete while any required field is being carried only in an email thread, an unlabelled photograph or a person's memory. Assign every open item to a named role with a due date.

## Limitations

This template is a document-control aid. It is not a certificate, warranty, inspection report or statement of compliance. Project requirements, applicable regulations, exposure, fire strategy, structural design, substrate, joints, sealants and fixing details must be reviewed by the responsible project professionals.
