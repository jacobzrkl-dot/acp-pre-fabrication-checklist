# ACP delivery-to-storage handover checklist

Use this short checklist after the receiving team has made its delivery record and before protected ACP packs are transferred to the agreed storage area. It is a project-communication aid, not a lifting plan, safety procedure, technical approval or installation release.

## 1. Keep the pack identifiable

- [ ] The pack or panel group can still be connected to the delivery note, panel schedule or agreed project reference.
- [ ] The person coordinating the transfer knows the intended storage location.
- [ ] The receiving record can be found again after the pack leaves the delivery area.
- [ ] Any temporary identification follows the project’s own document-control process.

## 2. Preserve the handover facts

- [ ] Relevant visible observations are recorded before the pack is redistributed or unwrapped.
- [ ] The observation names the specific pack or panel group rather than a general delivery.
- [ ] A visible-condition record is not treated as proof of cause or responsibility.
- [ ] Questions requiring a decision are escalated through the project’s agreed process.

## 3. Distinguish the next decisions

- [ ] The team does not treat unloading as permission to install.
- [ ] Current drawings, system details and release steps remain the reference for installation.
- [ ] Storage conditions are agreed separately from the delivery and unloading record.
- [ ] The next responsible team can identify what must be checked before panels are opened, moved again or installed.

## 4. Close the transfer

- [ ] The receiving lead knows which packs reached the intended area.
- [ ] The storage team can identify each group using the agreed records.
- [ ] Any unresolved difference has a clear owner and next action.
